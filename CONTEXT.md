# Context & Ubiquitous Language

## Core Concepts

### Directory
The public, searchable index of musicians who have taken public stances, signed statements, or engaged in boycotts in solidarity with Palestinians.

### Artist Profile
A published canonical record representing a musical artist or group, containing verified evidence records, campaign associations, research metadata, and audit dates.

### Evidence Record
A single verified instance of public action or statement attributed to an artist. Must contain a summary, source publication or platform label, direct source URL, classification category, and timestamp.

### Evidence Tier / Category
The archival classification assigned to an artist or evidence record based on primary source evidence:
- **G-D (Direct anti-genocide)**: Direct personal statement explicitly naming or addressing the genocide.
- **G-C (Collective anti-genocide)**: Signatory to a collective statement, campaign letter, or boycott against the genocide.
- **S (Solidarity / ceasefire)**: Broader humanitarian solidarity, ceasefire appeals, or relief benefit concerts.
- **H (Historical-only)**: Documented solidarity actions taken prior to October 2023.

### Campaign
An organized collective movement, open letter, or cultural boycott network (e.g., No Music for Genocide, Musicians for Palestine, Strike Germany, Artists4Ceasefire) with published attribution of artist participation.

### Research Baseline & Snapshot
The baseline compilation date and package metadata from which the directory data was imported. Does not denote the date an artist made a statement.

### Audit Log (Changes)
The append-only operational log recording editorial events (import, review, publish, withdraw) maintaining data provenance.
