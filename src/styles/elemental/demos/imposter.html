Imposter [data-imposter]

Imposter positions an overlay element directly centered over its parent container (absolute) or viewport (fixed). When contain is enabled, it bounds the element to calc(100% - (var(--imposter-margin) * 2)) and enables internal scrolling (overflow: auto) when content overflows.
Containment limit: calc(100% - 2 * margin)
